# Urban growth in Argentina's agglomerations, 1990-2020

How the 15 EPH urban agglomerations of Argentina grew between 1990 and 2020, measured with the classes of the **Atlas of Urban Expansion** (Angel et al. 2016) on the **GISD30** impervious-surface record (Landsat, 30 m).

The question behind every page: **how much does it change the picture to count the fringe open space** (the 100 m ring of unbuilt land around urban and suburban built-up) as part of the city?

## In one paragraph

Across the 15 agglomerations, built-up land inside the urban extent grew from 757 km² in 1990 to 1,645 km² in 2020 (2.62 % a year); without Gran Buenos Aires, from 757 to 1,645 km² (2.62 % a year). The fringe open space is 30.2 % of the total urban extent in 2020, and between 23.6 % and 39.8 % city by city. Counting it adds 31-66 % to the area of each city, but moves its 1990-2020 growth rate by less than 0.2 points a year in 7 of the 15.
