Open land beyond the ring is grouped in patches (pixels touching by a side). A patch up to this size, in a tract at or above this percentile of the city's tract densities (persons per km² urbanized), turns into built-up. Larger patches stay open. Water never fills. Narrow gaps: open pixels inside a gap between built pixels (the 60 m rule of the single-city viewer), whatever the size of their patch.

floor: percentile
always keep if built around ≥

Floor = this percentile of the city's tracts' persons per km² of built-up. A tract below the floor keeps only persons ÷ floor km² of built-up: the pixels with most built-up within 150 m first. Solid fabric above the second setting (industry, airports) is always kept.
