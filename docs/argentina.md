## How much the fringe open space matters

### On the size of the city: a lot

In 2020 the fringe is between 21.8 % and 39.8 % of the urban extent; counting it adds 28-66 % to the area of everything else. Anything divided by the urban extent (population density, share of the city in a given condition) moves by that much depending on whether the fringe is in.

![Fringe share 1990 and 2020]({{ '/assets/img/argentina_franja.png' | relative_url }})

### On growth: little in most, a lot in a few

Counting the fringe moves the 1990-2020 growth rate by less than 0.2 points a year in 15 of the 31 agglomerations. It lowers growth most where the city fills in its old fringe ([Posadas]({{ '/agglomerations/posadas/' | relative_url }}), [Río Cuarto]({{ '/agglomerations/rio_cuarto/' | relative_url }}), [Jujuy - Palpalá]({{ '/agglomerations/jujuy/' | relative_url }})) and raises it where new scattered patches add new fringe ([Gran Santa Fe]({{ '/agglomerations/santa_fe/' | relative_url }}), [Gran La Plata]({{ '/agglomerations/la_plata/' | relative_url }}), [Gran Rosario]({{ '/agglomerations/rosario/' | relative_url }})).
